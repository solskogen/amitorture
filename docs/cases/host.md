# host (pseudo cases, made by the runner)

Not a disk module: the runner turns some host records (the screenshots and audio captures the guest asks for, docs/schema.md §5.1, CONTRACT §13) into pseudo cases, so they are judged and fingerprinted like guest cases (runner/amitorture/cases.py). They are not counted in the per-entry and per-module case totals.
Their failures do count toward the disk and entry verdict: a zero guest
`done.fail` does not override a failed audio measurement.

The former `speed_<insn>` cases of the hostspeed module are retired: the bench disk measures speed
(docs/BENCH.md). Baselines that still hold them report them as `retired`.

| case | intent | rule | expected | a failure usually means |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `shot_<tag>` | the picture of an acknowledged screenshot, on cycle-exact entries only (elsewhere the frame timing is not exact, so screenshots are kept but not compared) | the same machine, disk and seed give the same frame | always pass; the fingerprint is the PNG's sha256, so a baseline/check compares the picture | (a `check` regression: the picture changed) |
| `tone_<tag>` | the frequency of a tone the guest played (Paula `aud0`-`aud3`, sound boards), measured in the captured audio, on every entry that captured audio | the expected frequency the guest gives in the audio marks (`expect_hz`, `freq_mhz`, or clock, period and samples) and its tolerance `tol_pct` | pass within the tolerance, on the expected output channel (`side`: left, right, or both for a mono source) if given; the fingerprint is the expected frequency plus the deviation rounded to the tolerance, so it is stable on every core | fail: the wrong period or sample rate reaches the output, the wrong output channel, or a silent channel; untestable: no audio in the segment, or a tone whose length is not what the guest played (an emulator running slower than real time stretches the capture) |
| `cet_<name>`: `cet_chip_read`, `cet_move_reg`, `cet_lsl_l_8`, `cet_dbra`, `cet_move_pair`, `cet_move_pair_dep`, `cet_chip_write_nodma`, `cet_chip_write_6pl`, `cet_chip_write_spr`, `cet_chip_write_6pl_spr`, `cet_chip_write_3pl` | CPU clocks per loop iteration of a cetiming perf record (docs/cases/cetiming.md has one line per loop) on the cycle-exact entries with a real-hardware reference (A1200: 68020/68EC020, multiplier 4, AGA) | clk_per_iter = ticks x 5 x cpu_multiplier / iterations, against the real machine's value in `CET_REFS` (runner/amitorture/cases.py), +-2 % (`CET_TOL`) | pass within the tolerance; the fingerprint is `{st}` only, clk_per_iter and ratio (to the real machine) are its `timing`. Not host-relative: the emulated CPU and CIA share one clock | fail: the cycle-exact core charges the wrong time for an instruction, a cache-hit fetch, a chip-bus access or a DMA slot (docs/cases/cetiming.md) |
